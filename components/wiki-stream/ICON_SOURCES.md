# Architecture diagram icons

Brand SVG paths in `brand-icons.json` come from the Simple Icons project:
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (CC0-1.0).

Retrieved 2026-10-27 from `develop/icons/{wikipedia,apachekafka,supabase,vercel,render}.svg`.
Brand names and marks identify the services used in the project; they remain the
property of their respective owners. Processing, dashboard and rejection symbols
in `ArchitectureIcon.tsx` are native SVG illustrations created for this diagram.

All paths are bundled locally; there are no runtime image/CDN requests.
